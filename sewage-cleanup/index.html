Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Across Indianapolis, Indianapolis Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Indianapolis

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
